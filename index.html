Cherished Life Ministries (CLM) is a registered community-based organization in Rukungiri District, Uganda. We believe every person deserves the opportunity to live with dignity and hope.

Guided by Christian values, we work with communities to strengthen families, nurture wellbeing and create lasting opportunities for a brighter future.
